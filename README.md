
# Telco Customer Churn Analysis & Prediction

An end-to-end data analytics and machine learning project focused on analyzing customer churn for a telecommunications company. This project explores **7,043 customer records** to identify churn patterns, understand major factors associated with customer attrition, and generate insights that can help improve customer retention.

Python was used for data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, and machine learning. SQL was used for data calculations, while Excel and Tableau were used for data visualization and reporting.

---

## Project Overview

Customer churn is a major challenge for subscription-based businesses. Understanding why customers leave helps companies improve customer satisfaction, strengthen retention strategies, and reduce revenue loss.

In this project, I analyzed the Telco Customer Churn dataset to:

- Understand overall customer churn patterns.
- Identify customer segments with higher churn rates.
- Analyze the relationship between contract type and customer churn.
- Study how customer tenure influences churn.
- Investigate services associated with customer retention.
- Identify potential revenue at risk.
- Build a machine learning model to predict customer churn.

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Collection:** Worked with the Telco Customer Churn dataset containing 7,043 customer records.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Processed the dataset using Python and prepared it for analysis.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Examined customer characteristics, service usage, contract types, tenure, and churn patterns.
4. **SQL Analysis:** Performed data calculations to support the analysis.
5. **Machine Learning:** Built a model to predict customer churn.
6. **Visualization:** Created dashboards in Tableau to communicate important findings.
7. **Business Insights:** Identified customer segments and potential areas for retention improvement.

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Customer Distribution

Analyzed the distribution of customers across different categories and service segments.

### 2. Churn Rate by Contract

Examined churn patterns across different contract types to understand which customer groups may require stronger retention efforts.

### 3. Churn Rate by Tenure

Analyzed the relationship between customer tenure and churn to identify potential retention opportunities at different stages of the customer lifecycle.

### 4. Technical Support and Services

Investigated churn patterns across service categories, including technical support and online security.

### 5. Revenue at Risk

Analyzed monthly revenue associated with customers who may be lost due to churn.

### 6. Machine Learning-Based Churn Prediction

Used Python to build a machine learning model and generate customer churn predictions and risk-related outputs.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following areas can be considered for improving customer retention:

- **Contract Strategy:** Focus on retaining customers who are more likely to churn under flexible contract arrangements.
- **Early Customer Engagement:** Strengthen onboarding and engagement during the initial customer lifecycle.
- **Customer Support:** Review support services and identify opportunities to improve customer experience.
- **Service Improvement:** Evaluate service categories associated with higher churn.
- **Retention Campaigns:** Use churn probability and risk segments to prioritize customers for targeted retention campaigns.
- **Revenue Protection:** Monitor revenue associated with high-risk customers and prioritize retention efforts accordingly.
